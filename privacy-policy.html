Wiz Bill Box – Privacy Policy

Wiz Labs · Effective 10 November 2026
In short: your bills are stored on your phone and in your own Google Drive. Wiz Labs has no server and never receives your bills, photos or account details.

What the app stores

When you add a bill, the app saves its photos and the details you type (store name, product, invoice date and any optional details such as amount, invoice number, category, payment mode, warranty date, GSTIN and notes) in the app's private storage on your phone.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to use Google Drive with the drive.file scope. This lets it create and manage only the files and folders it creates, in a folder called “Wiz Bill Box” in your Drive. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

The app also reads your Google account's email address and name, only to show you which account you're signed in with.

Bills are sent directly from your phone to Google's servers over an encrypted connection. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • No Wiz Labs server, account or cloud: we never receive your data.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you tap “Camera” to photograph a bill. When you pick from your gallery, the system photo picker gives the app only the pictures you choose.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting a bill removes it from your phone and moves its folder to the bin in your Google Drive.
  • “Sign out” removes all bills from the phone and revokes the app's access to your Drive. Bills already in Drive stay there until you delete the “Wiz Bill Box” folder.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account › Security › Third-party connections.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
