Wiz Bill Box

Snap it. Save it. Find it.

Wiz Bill Box keeps every bill, invoice and receipt in one place, safely in your own Google Drive. Take a photo, add the store, product and invoice date, and you're done. Sign in on any phone and your bills come back.

Features

  • Photograph bills or pick them from your gallery
  • Optional amount (₹), invoice number, category, payment mode, warranty, GSTIN and notes
  • Saved to a "Wiz Bill Box" folder in your Google Drive and restored after Google sign-in
  • Search, filters and "warranty ending soon" reminders
  • Works offline; no ads, no tracking

Your data

The app uses the Google Drive drive.file permission: it can only see the files it creates. Wiz Labs has no server and never receives your bills. Read the privacy policy.
