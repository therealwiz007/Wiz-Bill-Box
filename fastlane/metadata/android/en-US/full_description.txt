Lost a bill right when you needed a warranty claim? Wiz Bill Box keeps every
bill, receipt and warranty in your own Google Drive, and reminds you before a
warranty ends.

SAVE IN SECONDS
• Snap a bill with the camera, pick photos from the gallery, or add a PDF bill
• Store, product and date are all you need; add amount (₹), invoice number,
  GSTIN, payment mode, category and notes if you like
• Store names you used before are suggested as you type

YOUR DRIVE, YOUR DATA
• Each bill is saved to My Drive › Wiz Bill Box in its own folder
• The app can only see files it created (Google's drive.file access)
• Sign in on a new phone and every bill comes back
• Works offline first: bills upload when you are online

NEVER MISS A WARRANTY
• See active, expiring and expired warranties at a glance
• Reminders 30, 7 and 1 day before a warranty ends (you choose)

FIND ANY BILL
• Search by store, product, invoice number, notes or GSTIN
• Filter by 12 categories, sort by date, amount or store
• Export all bills to a CSV file

Made by Wiz Labs. No Wiz Labs account and no Wiz Labs server: your bills
stay in your Google Drive. No ads are shown in this version.
